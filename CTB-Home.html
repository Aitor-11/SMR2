About CTB

The Centre for Biomedical Technology is a research and technology centre of the Technical University of Madrid that brings together researchers from different disciplines on biomedical technologies, in collaboration with other external institutions, in order to address major challenges in Biomedicine, facilitate technology transfer to industry, and train new researchers in this field.


Discover more →

Our Mission

Address major challenges that scientists are facing today in Biomedicine and Health, requiring stable and interdisciplinary collaboration that includes both basic and translational research to develop technologies transferred to industry.
